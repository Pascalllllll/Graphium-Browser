# Privacy

Graphium has no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nobody, including
the people who make Graphium, can see what you do in it.

## What stays on your computer

- History, settings, shortcuts on the new tab page and site permissions.
- Cookies, saved site data and the browser cache.
- Downloaded files, in the folder you choose.

You can delete all of it from **Menu → Settings → Clear history, cookies and cache**,
or have it done every time you quit under *Advanced*.
Private windows keep nothing after you close them.

## What Graphium sends on its own

- **Ad block lists.** Every few days Graphium downloads the latest ad and
  tracker lists from Ghostery's public server. The request contains nothing
  about you or the pages you visit.
- **Search.** What you type in the address bar goes to the search engine you
  picked in Settings (Google by default), the same as in any browser.
- **Shortcut icons.** The new tab page loads each shortcut's icon from that
  site.

Everything else is a page you asked to open. Websites you visit can still see
your IP address and anything you give them, as in any browser.
